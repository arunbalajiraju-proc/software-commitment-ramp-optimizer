# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-08-03

- added a non-compensating commitment-readiness gate;
- added accountable owners and evidence references to the decision record;
- added downloadable full, phased, and true-down supplier pricing structures;
- added evaluation of actual supplier offers on common seeded demand scenarios;
- added post-award usage reconciliation and contract-action guidance;
- added a Toronto readiness reconstruction and labelled retrospective boundary;
- expanded the Streamlit workflow from planning to approval, sourcing, and control;
- expanded automated coverage from 15 to 24 tests; and
- documented organizational pilot and enterprise-hardening requirements.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-08-03

- replaced the model console with a guided procurement planner;
- added plain-language inputs, buying schedule, price ceiling, and negotiation plan; and
- added a rollout-completion plateau to the adoption model.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-01

- initial transparent optimizer, Toronto public case, CLI, dashboard, and publication pack.
